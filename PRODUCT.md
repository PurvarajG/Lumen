# Lumen Presenter — product context

## Who and where

Sabha/mandir AV volunteers of mixed experience, operating live in a dim booth or at the side of the hall, often on a MacBook with one projector or TV as the audience output and sometimes a stage confidence monitor. They run kirtans (Kirtan Muktavali), scripture readings (Vachanamrut, Swamini Vato, Satsang Diksha and other books downloaded in-app from anirdesh.com), announcements, countdowns and occasional video. They are watched by a hall full of people: a wrong slide on screen is the failure that matters.

## Primary job

Find content and send it live fast and safely: search a kirtan or passage → preview → go live in seconds, with Next/Previous obvious and impossible to confuse with editing. Preparing shows (playlists, themes, slide editing) is the secondary job and happens before the event.

## Product truths

- Gujarati and English are first-class; most content is Gujarati. Text must shape correctly (conjuncts, matras) on outputs and previews.
- Preview and live are visibly separate. Editing never changes what is on air until the operator triggers it.
- Nothing irreversible without intent: clear/blackout are one keystroke but clearly labelled; destructive edits confirm or undo.
- Works offline once books are downloaded. No account, no cloud.
- Book texts are © their publishers; Lumen downloads them on the user's Mac for personal use and never bundles them.

## Success

A volunteer who has never used Lumen can, within five minutes, open a playlist, find "Vachanamrut Gadhada I-1" or a kirtan by its first line, preview it and run it slide by slide, clear the screen and start a countdown — without asking for help.

## Anti-goals

Marketing-style UI, animation that delays a cue, hidden modes, tiny hit targets, jargon-only labels, any design where LIVE and PREVIEW can be mistaken.
